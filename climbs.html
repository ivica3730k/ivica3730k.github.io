How this was built

Climbs were detected by scanning each ride's GPS+altimeter trace for sustained uphill segments — minimum 50 m of vertical gain over at least 400 m, at an average gradient of 2.5% or more. Elevation was smoothed with a 15-point rolling mean to filter GPS/barometric noise.

Repeated climbs were grouped by clustering start coordinates within 150 m. VAM (vertical ascent metres per hour) is the standard climbing performance metric — solid as a fitness proxy on climbs steeper than ~5%, somewhat noisier on gentler grades where wind and effort variability matter more.

27 of 213 rides had no usable GPS+elevation data (indoor/trainer rides) and were excluded.
